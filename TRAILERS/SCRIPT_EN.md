# Lactose — detailed trailer script

Target: 52 seconds, 16:9, 60 FPS. Tone: dry comedy with clearly demonstrated survival systems.

## Capture preparation

- Use the English release and default configuration.
- Setup commands: `/function lactose:config/default`, `/function lactose:admin/reset_player`, and `/function lactose:stats/show`.
- Prepare regular milk, ingredients for Lactose-Free Milk, ingredients for Lactose-Free Cake, and a nearby cow.
- Reset player data before the first take so reaction growth is predictable.
- Do not use or show a crafting function; both recipes are normal crafting-table recipes.

## Required proof shots

- Reaction state is per player.
- Stronger reaction follows repeated milk use.
- Statistics and advancement UI come from the release.
- Both safe recipes are shown at a crafting table.
- No obsolete `lactose:craft` command appears.

## Reset

Use `/function lactose:admin/reset_player` before each reaction sequence. Clear the sidebar after statistics shots with `/function lactose:stats/clear_sidebar`.

## Deliverables

- Full English trailer.
- 15-second comedy cut: confident sip → reaction → cow → lactose-free recipe → title.
- Thumbnail candidate: milk bucket, cake, and a judging cow.
