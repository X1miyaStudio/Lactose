Lactose turns milk from a universal cure into a compact per-player survival system. Drinking milk builds a temporary reaction, repeated drinking raises the danger, and recovery gradually improves tolerance.

## Main systems

- Separate reaction state for every player in singleplayer and multiplayer.
- Reaction decay and recovery over time.
- Anti-spam streak tracking for repeated milk use.
- Tolerance and adaptation as players recover.
- Optional challenge mode for faster escalation.
- Lucky digestion, dairy disaster, and nearby-cow events.
- A custom dairy advancement tree.
- Player statistics and leaderboard commands.
- Lactose-free milk and lactose-free cake recipes.

## Play styles

- `/function lactose:config/soft` — shorter, gentler reactions.
- `/function lactose:config/default` — balanced default behavior.
- `/function lactose:config/spicy` — louder and more dangerous milk mistakes.
- `/function lactose:config/challenge_on` — faster reaction growth.
- `/function lactose:config/challenge_off` — disable challenge mode.

Configuration commands are intended for operators. The selected configuration applies to the world.

## Lactose-free crafting

Lactose-Free Milk is a shapeless recipe using a milk bucket, honey bottle, wheat, and sugar.

Lactose-Free Cake uses the normal cake layout: three milk buckets on top, sugar around one egg in the middle, and three wheat on the bottom. It is crafted directly at a normal crafting table; no crafting function or inventory command is required.

The exact 1.21.11 recipe grids are included in the gallery.

## Statistics and administration

- `/function lactose:stats/show` — show the player's current statistics.
- `/function lactose:stats/top_milk` — display milk usage.
- `/function lactose:stats/top_food` — display tracked dairy food.
- `/function lactose:stats/top_reaction` — display highest reactions.
- `/function lactose:stats/clear_sidebar` — clear the statistics sidebar.
- `/function lactose:admin/help` — list administration commands.
- `/function lactose:admin/status` — display current world configuration.
- `/function lactose:admin/reset_player` — reset the executing player's data.
- `/function lactose:admin/reset_all` — reset all player data.
- `/function lactose:uninstall` — remove Lactose objectives before uninstalling.

## Installation

1. Download one language version.
2. Place the ZIP in `<world>/datapacks/`.
3. Enter the world or run `/reload`.
4. Choose a configuration or keep the balanced default.

Do not install the English and Russian ZIPs together; they are alternative localizations.
